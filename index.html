Waiting for a chain.
Alfred will pick up CH macros like GG 001 CH -- Tank and rampage macros like GG AAA RCH -- Tank. Start with !startchain.

CH uses numbers (GG 001 CH -- Tank). Rampage uses RCH and letters (GG AAA RCH -- Tank). !take takes the next free CH number. !take 001 and !skip 001 go to CH; !take AAA and !skip AAA go to rampage. Add a name to place someone else: !take 001 Portlia or !take Portlia. Bare !skip / !back apply to every chain you are on. !startchain and !stopchain arm and stop CH and rampage together. The clock starts on the first CH or RCH. !startchain also shows a Chain is starting alert.

Every chain Alfred watches becomes a session. Sessions end on !stopchain or !reset-chain, or on their own after three quiet minutes. Score starts at 100 and drops for drift off the beat, missed turns, and CHes on the wrong target.
